Copy into the episode folder. Mark each item `pass`, `needs work`, `not checked`, or `not applicable`, with evidence or notes. A checkbox alone is not proof. Recheck changed material after revisions.

## Story and experience

- [ ] The opening makes the situation intriguing; the extraordinary element supports the owner's intended unbelievable experience.
- [ ] The survival threat, world rule, character decision and immediate consequence are understandable without prior episodes.
- [ ] Narration, images, captions and cause-and-effect agree; factual survival claims are verified or clearly fictional.
- [ ] Character, location and inventory match the brief; the episode has an earned payoff.
- [ ] Key constraints are visible without relying on narration alone, and each important beat has a clear action or visual change.

## First-view rough cut

Record the composition ID and revision. Show the temporary cut once at phone size and normal speed to someone unfamiliar with the brief. Ask for their own explanation before sharing the intended answer:

1. “Kenapa [nama tokoh] akhirnya [aksi yang berhasil]?”
2. “Bagian mana yang paling kamu ingat atau kamu suka?”
3. “Ada bagian yang bikin bingung?”

Record their words, the earliest confusing beat and what made an impression. Link any revision and retest result. If working alone, label the observations producer self-review and record independent viewer evidence as pending.

## Picture, sound and accessibility

- [ ] Character model sheet and seated pose inspected at full size and phone size. Head, jaw, neck, collar, shoulders, torso and seat connect coherently across the views actually used.
- [ ] Representative neutral hold, turn and story action played in real time. Character remains appealing and recognizable, with no obvious deformations, sliding joints, broken contact or abrupt pose changes. Note the test interval and any unresolved defect.
- [ ] Full composition played in real time, including scene boundaries and final frame; no broken assets or unintended blank frames.
- [ ] Phone-size readability, contrast, edge clearance, diagrams and localized labels checked; platform UI preview checked when available.
- [ ] Indonesian narration is intelligible; pronunciation, levels, music and effects checked by listening.
- [ ] Main narrator matches the selected masculine Indonesian voice profile and reference sample; pacing, pronunciation and joins are consistent. If no voice has been selected or listening was unavailable, record this as not checked.
- [ ] Captions match final audio, are aligned to actual speech and readable, and have been checked for spelling and clipping. Text-length estimates and automated transcripts alone are not proof of sync.

## Technical, rights and delivery

- [ ] Correct composition ID, dimensions, FPS and duration; no unexpected console or render errors.
- [ ] Relevant lint/build checks run; failures and pre-existing issues are recorded.
- [ ] Sources, licenses, attribution and voice permissions documented; no unresolved rights for publication.
- [ ] Reproduction commands identify paths and versions and do not silently overwrite retained assets; environment-specific archived scripts are labeled.
- [ ] AI disclosure and current platform rules checked for the actual content when publishing.
- [ ] If an export was requested, the actual exported file was played through with picture, audio and caption sync checked.

## Final pre-publish checklist

- [ ] **Cover at 00:00.2.** Check that the 3–4 word opening title is readable at phone size and sits within the upper-center 3:4 crop in the three-column profile preview. Check that it runs from 00:00.0 to 00:01.8.
- [ ] **Shot repeats.** Review the cut at normal speed. Confirm zero mood-breaking repeats and check the validator’s duplicate image path result.
- [ ] **HUD and caption numbers.** Compare each on-screen number against the corresponding HUD value and formatting, including percentages and clock times.
- [ ] **Caption safe zone.** Check caption clearance from UI, edges and important picture details in the actual platform preview.
- [ ] **Reveal SFX hit.** Listen to the final mix and verify the effect lands on the intended reveal frame without masking speech.

For each, record the time, what is visible, the inference it supports and any plausible misreading. Replay with sound and captions at normal speed. Identify one motion cue by its initiating frame, contact or change, and the first frame where the result is readable. Revise and replay any cue whose cause is hard to see.
