## Studio v3 · Arsip Terakhir

Use the [v3 style guide](STYLE_GUIDE.md#studio-v3--arsip-terakhir) and [studio contracts](agents/README.md). Start from [Arsip Terakhir](arcs/03-arsip-terakhir/arc.json). Keep physical episode folders directly under `episodes/<slug>/`, cite episodes by their full path (`episodes/007-grup-ronda-rt07` vs `episodes/arc1/005-suara-dari-rumah-kosong`), and set `pipelineVersion: 3` in `chat-script.json`.

**No narrator.** Each video is a found-phone or found-footage archive. The viewer reconstructs what happened from artifacts: a group chat, short character voice notes, photos sent in the chat, CCTV stills, call logs, notifications, and status lines such as `"terakhir dilihat 03.12"`. Nobody explains the story.

- **Length & specs:** **45–75 s**, **1080 × 1920**, **30 fps**.
- **Hook:** The first message or artifact is on screen at **00:00.0**, readable within **2 s**, and contains an impossible detail (for example, `"Siapa yang barusan ketuk pintu kamar 4? Kita kan cuma bertiga."`).
- **Beat shape:** Normal → odd detail → escalation → silence or reveal. **No rewind loops.**
- **Fantasy rule & series universe:** Each episode is a standalone story (new setting, characters, and **one local entity or rule**, never explained aloud; the viewer infers it). The one series-level constant is **"Tanpa Nama"** — a recurring presence (not a second explained rule) who always sends the final artifact of every archive using the fixed display name `"Tanpa Nama"`, one fixed grey default avatar, no real or realistic phone number, and the original synthesized Tanpa Nama signature notification sound (played ONLY when a Tanpa Nama event arrives). Tanpa Nama is never explained, never the solution, and never talks about himself; he may also appear once earlier, subtly (call log, CCTV corner, forwarded tag), as a cross-episode clue. Track every on-screen Tanpa Nama appearance in [arcs/03-arsip-terakhir/lore.md](arcs/03-arsip-terakhir/lore.md) without inventing off-screen lore.
- **Fair-play clue:** Plant at least one visual clue in the **first 40%** (for example, inside a sent photo) that explains the ending so viewers rewatch to find it. Record it in the brief's **Clue Ledger** with timestamps and message IDs.
- **Survival angle:** Someone survives (or nearly does) because they noticed the clue or obeyed the rule. Never present this as real-world safety advice.
- **Ending:** The final artifact is always sent by **Tanpa Nama** and recasts everything — an unread/unplayed last message, a new voice note, or a photo that should not exist. No `"Kamu berhasil bertahan hidup."` No recap.
- **Language:** Everyday spoken Indonesian chat style (singkatan, typo ringan, `"wkwk"`, lowercase), consistent per character, with a distinct texting voice for each character. Ground settings locally: kos, minimarket shift malam, ronda, KKN, rumah nenek, ojol, etc.
- **Audio:** Voice notes are short in-world character lines (**2–6 s** each) made with TTS. Use the masculine Indonesian voice for one male character (a character, not a narrator). Add notification sounds (normal UI pops for characters; the original synthesized Tanpa Nama signature sound ONLY for Tanpa Nama events), typing, ambience, and deliberate silence. **No music that explains the mood.**
- **Visuals:** Mostly Remotion-rendered UI built in code (all motion driven by `useCurrentFrame()`, no CSS animations; overlay transitions `<= 6` frames or hard cuts so readable text never appears doubled). Generate **3–6 amateur phone photos or CCTV stills** per episode (noise, flash, low light — not illustrations). Keep everything **gore-free**.
- **Series identity, labels & brand safety:** Public numbering restarts at `ARSIP #001` (`episodes/007-grup-ronda-rt07` = `ARSIP #001`; folder names and composition IDs stay unchanged). Every video displays a solid series identity strip directly under the chat header reading **`ARSIP #NNN · @skenarium`** (`>= 42 px`, inside the critical safe zone; `archiveNumber` integer required in `chat-script.json`, handle fixed series-wide in one constant). By owner decision on 2026-11-24, there is **no on-screen `FIKSI` tag** (the account bio states fiction); instead, fiction disclosure moves to upload: every upload caption must include **`#fiksi`** and **`#ceritahorror`**, and the platform's built-in **AI-generated-content label** must be switched on at upload. Use a generic, original chat app design — **do not copy WhatsApp, Telegram, or Line branding, colors, or icons**. No real people, real phone numbers, or real place names that could defame anyone.
- **Captions & safe zone:** **No narration captions.** On-screen text is the chat; voice notes get a short subtitle under the bubble. All text must be readable on a phone (**minimum ~42 px** at 1080 width) and stay inside the platform UI safe zone (**avoid the bottom ~22% and the right ~12%**).
- **Reading pace:** Hold each new message for at least **~180 ms per word + 600 ms** (minimum **1.2 s**). Scroll the chat like a real phone.
