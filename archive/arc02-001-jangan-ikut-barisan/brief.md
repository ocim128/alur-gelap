## Spatial & Camera Layout — lock before prompts

All geography is outdoors, at night, in heavy rain; no interior or vehicle shots. Orient every establishing camera **looking east** down the path: concrete perimeter wall on frame-left/north, wet gravel path in the middle, tracks on frame-right/south. A waist-high steel fence runs between path and tracks, then ends at the fork. The evacuation entrance is a short right/southeast spur through a **fixed open gap** in a separate tall perimeter fence: no moving gate leaves or ambiguous hinges. Beyond the gap, a straight paved strip and the line lead to a lit station entrance, 15–20 m away. Its canopy covers the entrance only: rain falls outdoors or is visible beyond the canopy, never through it. The green `POS EVAKUASI` sign is fixed above the entrance; a red emergency lamp on the gap post lights only the imagined bad branch. No glass or reflections are plot-bearing.

At the fork the chalk warning is on the near face of the left wall, about chest height. Guard is left/near the wall, flashlight in her right hand; companion is one step to her right, grasping her left sleeve; gap and the queue are ahead/right, dark path continues ahead/left along the wall. The bad branch follows them right through the gap; the rewind restores this exact pose, positions, props, lighting and uninjured state. The survival path goes left and stays outside the perimeter fence. The wall continues along the path past a drainage channel crossing it; a single loose narrow plank spans the channel perpendicular to the route. Water flows away from camera/southeast to a visible bend. Both characters start on the near bank; guard crosses first; dropped flashlight travels downstream, not into the track bed. Companion crosses only after she climbs out. Beyond the crossing the wall ends at a square corner; the guard and companion stop just before it. The figure stands on the **same path ahead beyond the corner**, facing their direction with its head initially turned back toward the distant station. A sliver of cold sky gives enough light to read its silhouette and chalk without a replacement flashlight. No teleport is asserted.

Use a low near-ground view for boots and warning, a clearly oriented wide for the fork, then camera positions on the same path side of the barriers. Reverse angles must retain established left/right geography by explicitly marking the reversed view. Final close-up is from the survivors' position looking east around the corner; the figure's left hand is on viewer-right in that head-on view. Both survivors remain behind the corner and the figure remains ahead on the path. No other unmarked people beyond the queue, guard, companion and final figure. Maintain the flashlight's right-hand position until it falls, the plank's displaced position, wet knee and HUD change through the rest of the cut.

## Script and decision

- **Engine:** Branch-Rewind: a plausible, lit evacuation route tempts both people; the guard enters, the queue crushes the companion and seizes the guard. This entire red/amber branch is a possible outcome, not accepted continuity. At **87.0–87.4 s**, a `branchRewind` tape/static snap returns to the established fork, with all harm and prop changes from that branch undone. The guard instead chooses the dark path. Her knee is injured and the flashlight is lost in the surviving timeline; the companion still follows.
- **Opening:** “Di luar stasiun, jangan ikut barisan itu.” Picture simultaneously shows boots, torch, warning, the lit route and the distant line; this is a concrete warning for a first-time viewer. No recap or numbered lecture.
- **Blind spot:** avoiding the queue prevents its attack but does not make the route safe. The silent figure ahead holds chalk like the warning's material; this is a visual implication, **not proof** it wrote that particular warning. The quiet reveal belongs to this part; no later plot is prescribed.
- **Word and beat source:** [narasi.txt](narasi.txt) is the single-speaker narration only. Dialogue below is separately recorded and must never be fed into or parsed as the Investigator track.
