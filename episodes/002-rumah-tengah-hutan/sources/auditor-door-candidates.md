# Auditor-directed door continuity candidates

The 22 November image-stage audit found that the selected latch design does not visibly release the outward-opening rear door and that P09 confuses the front and rear entrances. The following revised mechanism is a **candidate production design**, not an approved update to the brief: a single intact horizontal bar connects a keeper bolted to the fixed left jamb/wall to a bracket bolted to the moving door leaf. The hinge is on the right when seen from inside. The bar's right tip ends within the moving leaf, clear of the right jamb. The bar breaks between the wall keeper and leaf bracket; the wall-held piece stays on the wall and the leaf-held piece clears the left jamb when the door swings outward. From the exterior the fixed keeper appears on the opposite side of the doorway.

The intended sequence must show the same attachment in setup and impact views, disconnected pieces in P20, and a stationary jamb keeper and clear moving leaf in P22–P23. P08 and P15 have already exhausted the image contract's two retries, so they cannot be regenerated within that limit; do not mark the batch passed while they retain incompatible geometry. Exact candidate prompts and their individual results are recorded in `auditor-door-generation.md`.
