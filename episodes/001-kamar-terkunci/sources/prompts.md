P05's far chair feet are partly obscured, so all-four-feet placement is not independently clear. P22 has both feet on the chair seat, but the chair/window alignment should be checked at full size in context. P04, P15, P20 and P23 show recognizable exterior settings; anatomical hand assignments in side views P15 remain difficult to verify.
3. **Camera vantage continuity — fail.** P08 reads like an enclosed interior rather than a clear view from the established outside corner. P04's simplified substitute does not establish the complete intended prop continuity. P23–P24 suggest the route outdoors, but independent geography review is pending.
4. **Barrier, reflection and overlay physics — fail.** P14's latch contact is undermined by stretched anatomy. P02's head and small hatch are difficult to read at phone size, and P03/P19 use oversized openings relative to the hand-sized specification; the sliding-shutter overlay has not been prototyped. P19's chair/towel position breaks the check. P17's bottom gap appears as an unbroken light strip without painted shoe shadows. The high window appears closed and flush through P21 where visible, and open in P22–P23; actual overlay and hinge behavior remain untested. No intentional silent clue is accented in these static pictures.
5. **Text and phone readability — fail.** No script-mandated Indonesian lettering was requested in the plates, and no painted clocks appear in P02, P03, P07 or P11. P07 is a static image. The P04 key/knife clue is absent and P02's peering figure is too small to assess confidently at phone size; no cold-viewer test has occurred.
