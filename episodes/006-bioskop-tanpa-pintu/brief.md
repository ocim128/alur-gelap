## Story and script

Keep the pronoun-free five-word hook, “Pintu keluar bioskop ini palsu.” If the Investigator master puts the hook over 3.0 s, the existing fallback removes `ini` between `bioskop` and `palsu` from speech and captions. Show rows multiplying within the opening three seconds while the screen holds. Introduce Ranto in the next spoken beat. Establish the locked balcony door, growing seating and loose screen in P02–P04. Show the two exit signs and 01:50 clock in P05 while Ranto’s promise is spoken. The first door attempt starts around 00:37 in the revised scratch cut; the fresh Investigator take determines production timing.

The auditorium rebuilds its rows, aisles and doors away from the screen. The red and side doors return Ranto to an elevated threshold over the same auditorium. The projector, stage and fabric screen are fixed. Turning off the projector triggers the rows to surge toward the balcony. The loose cloth and draft suggest space behind the screen; they do not prove an exit all the way outdoors. In the winning branch he keeps the beam lit, descends the rear stair, walks straight down the centre aisle, climbs onto the stage, tears through the fabric, turns right backstage and discovers the half-open service gate and dawn beyond it. The uninterrupted exterior brick wall silently pays off the false exit. His closing line expresses that he still has time for the 07:00 promise; it does not show the performance or establish that he has attended it.
