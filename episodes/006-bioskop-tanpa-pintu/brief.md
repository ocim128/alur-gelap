## Story and script

Keep the pronoun-free five-word hook, “Bioskop ini nggak punya pintu.” If the Investigator master puts the hook over 3.0 s, the existing fallback removes `ini` between `Bioskop` and `nggak` from speech and captions. Show rows multiplying within the opening three seconds while the screen holds. Introduce Ranto in the next spoken beat. Establish the locked balcony door, growing seating and loose screen in P02–P04. Show the two exit signs and 01:50 clock in P05 while Ranto’s promise is spoken. Bring the first door attempt in around 00:30 in the provisional cut.

The auditorium rebuilds its rows, aisles and doors away from the screen. The red and side doors return Ranto to an elevated threshold over the same auditorium. The projector, stage and fabric screen are fixed. Turning off the projector triggers the rows to surge toward the balcony. The loose cloth and draft show that there is space behind the screen. In the winning branch he keeps the beam lit, descends the rear stair, walks straight down the centre aisle, climbs onto the stage, tears through the fabric, turns right backstage and ducks under the half-open service gate into dawn. The uninterrupted exterior brick wall silently pays off the red door. His closing line pays off the 07:00 promise while the gate starts down.
