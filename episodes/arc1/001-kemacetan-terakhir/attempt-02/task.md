# Recreate Kemacetan Terakhir · attempt 02

Build a new, editable Remotion Studio preview of this Indonesian illustrated thriller. A viewer watching for the first time should understand why Damar stays in the car, believe for a moment that his choice worked, then recognize where the tapping comes from. The creative test is whether changing the pictures and their timing makes that story easier to follow.

## Art direction

Imagine the sequence as a short illustrated film seen from one red sedan. Use an angular silhouette for Damar's short dark hair, a creased green jacket and a pale shirt. Choose one distinctive feature for the sedan, such as a chipped side mirror, and carry it across angles. Establish the steering wheel, side window, front headrest and rear bench as spatial landmarks. A viewer should know where Damar is looking even when only his face and a sliver of the cabin are visible.

Build the dusk scene around a low amber light catching rooflines and glazing the windshield. At night, let a cool strip of window light pick out the edge of Damar's face and the seat fabric. Keep his eyes and the door handle legible. Vary the vehicle silhouettes and spacing with perspective. Reserve the clearest outline and strongest local contrast for the current story action. Prepare or draw the character, cabin, surroundings and key props in separable layers so an arm can pass behind a door frame and a shadow can cross the window independently. Original layered illustration or carefully drawn vector art can both achieve this.

## Shot and motion direction

Audio clip numbers identify the beats. Place each cue against the actual voice performance. Start by roughing out 00, 03–04 and 10–11 at phone size, then play their cuts before polishing the rest.

| Clips | What to stage and animate | What the audience should understand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00 | Open directly on the jam. A cropped bumper fills the near edge. Damar's red sedan sits boxed in nose and tail. Adjacent lanes form unbroken queues shrinking toward the smoky skyline. Wheels stay still while a person squeezes between mirrors. Cut closer on the same red mirror as Damar looks out. | By the opening two seconds, the cars visibly block every way forward. The person can move through a space the sedan cannot. |
| 01–03 | From inside the car, follow Damar's gaze to a runner through the side window. His fingers curl toward the handle. A second figure veers after the runner. Match the runner's direction across the cut. Return to the same hand as it draws back and presses the lock. Give the lock pin a visible down position. | He considered getting out. Seeing someone pursued changes what he does. Keep the locked state through the next shot. |
| 04–05 | Use three tactile inserts. The key turns and the gauge needle falls. His thumb moves the light switch and the glow leaves the bumper ahead. The radio dial clicks and the lit dial goes dark. Match the last lit shape to the red sedan's position in a wider night view. Let the fading sound resolve in the dark cabin. | One person intentionally makes this particular car quiet and dark. The viewer can still find it among the traffic. |
| 06 | Frame Damar, the window sill and a gap between parked cars in one profile. He lowers his head below the sill. Outside, a passing shoulder briefly blocks the window light and moves out of frame. Hold on his eyes before he sits up. | The threat passes within arm's reach on the outside. Damar believes hiding worked. |
| 07–08 | Bring each item from his lap into the same pool of light on its spoken phrase. Let the waterline tilt and settle halfway up the bottle. Turn the single pack until “Permen karet” reads. Wake the phone to a large 60% battery. Keep the active item clear of captions. Return to his loosening grip and the still side window as he settles in to wait. | Half a bottle, one pack, sixty percent. Viewers can read each item during playback. His relaxed posture sells the false relief. |
| 09 | Use the same phone to establish 03.00. Leave a pocket of silence. At the first tap Damar freezes. At the second his eyes search the side window. Hold the outside space empty. Place a subtle Indonesian caption for the tap if the sound carries information a muted viewer would miss. | The sound wakes him. Like Damar, we initially suspect something outside. Keep the rear bench obscured until his attention shifts. |
| 10–11 | From the side window, follow his eyeline up to the rear-view mirror. His eyes shift first, then his head. On the turn, cut past his shoulder. Keep the near headrest and rear cushion in the frame as a pale hand taps inside the cabin and the infected passenger leans into the strip of light. Hold the readable silhouette for roughly a second before cutting to black. End on the discovery. | One continuous cabin contains both figures. The passenger was already behind him. The reveal should still make sense with the audio muted. |

For the lock, try a short movement with a readable end state. At 30 fps, start twelve frames before contact with the fingers hovering over the pin, press it over four frames, then let the hand withdraw while the depressed pin remains visible for another twelve. Place the click on the frame the pin seats. Adjust the timing in playback. Give the person passing the window a different rhythm and let the mirror reveal hold still. Keep labels, audio cues and moving parts editable in named sequences or layers. Use the [local frame-timing examples](../../../../.agents/skills/remotion-markup/timing.md) when implementing the cues.

## Audio, captions and review

Use the spoken take as the authority for Indonesian captions. Align phrase starts and ends against the WAVs by listening or by correcting audio-derived timestamps. Pay special attention to “permen karet” and “enam puluh persen”. If a word is genuinely missing or unintelligible, repair only that line using the selected v4 settings, save the new take under the v2 asset path and document the change. Retiming the scene also means retiming its effects. Keep the tap and the lock click aligned with the visible contact.

1. Play the opening, the hand withdrawing and the final turn with audio muted and captions hidden at 270 × 480. Record a timestamp for the gridlock, the cause of Damar's decision and the interior reveal. If the image supports a different interpretation, revise the staging and replay it.
2. Restore sound and captions. Watch the whole episode at normal speed. Read the inventory once at phone size. Check caption onset against the spoken items and look for any cut that exposes the passenger before the intended reveal. Record actual observations, plus any listening or viewer check still pending.
3. Run `npm run lint` and `npm run build` from the parent. Deliver the exact Studio URL, composition ID, runtime, source and asset paths, source/rights notes, and check results in `handoff.md` beside this file. Include a phone-size contact sheet of opening, decision, inventory and payoff with actual timestamps, plus playback intervals for the lock and the final turn so the reviewer can judge motion.

The default deliverable is the playable Studio composition. If the owner also asks for an export, save `kemacetan-terakhir-v2.mp4` beside this task using the installed render guidance, then check the exported picture and audio.
