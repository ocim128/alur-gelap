  > - **Darto** spotted someone he thought was Hendra in Gang 2, took a photo of the motionless dark-jacketed figure, and was chased when it turned around and ran at him—his voice note cuts off abruptly mid-sentence while running and panting, implying he was caught too. His subsequent stiff text (`"bayu kamu di mana? jawab aku bayu"`) and unanswered call read as the entity using his identity/phone to bait Bayu.
- **Q4: What (probably) happened to the phone owner?**
