This file is the single canon ledger for recurring appearances of **Tanpa Nama** across standalone *Arsip Terakhir* episodes.

## Series-Level Rules (Locked 2026-11-24)

- **Standalone episodes:** Each archive has a fresh Indonesian setting, cast, and **one local entity or rule** (never explained aloud).
- **The one series constant — `Tanpa Nama`:**
  - Always sends the **final artifact** of every archive.
  - Fixed identity: display name **`Tanpa Nama`**, one fixed neutral-grey default silhouette avatar (`V3_TANPA_NAMA`), and **no real or realistic phone number**.
  - Signature audio cue: `public/sfx/shared/tanpa-nama-ping.wav` plays **only** when a `Tanpa Nama` event arrives.
  - Never explained, never the solution to the episode's local rule, and never talks about himself.
  - May also appear once earlier in an episode, subtly (e.g., call log, CCTV corner, forwarded tag), as a cross-episode clue.
- **On-screen canon only:** Record only what literally appears on screen in completed or approved scripts. Do not invent off-screen backstory or future lore.
